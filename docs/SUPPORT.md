# Leaf support

Leaf is a reading planner for iPhone and Apple Watch, made by Norvo Designs.

**Contact.** Email norvodesigns@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model and iOS
version, and what you were doing when something went wrong. We reply within a
few days.

## Common questions

**How does Leaf work out my plan?** From your reading speed, the minutes you read
each day and your reading days, Leaf works through your Reading and Up next
shelves day by day. That gives today's pages and a finish date for every book.
Change any of these and the plan reflows.

**My reading speed looks wrong.** In Settings, tap Retake the reading test. Every
session you finish also refines it.

**How do I move my library to a new iPhone?** Sign in to the same Apple Account
with iCloud on, and your library syncs to the new iPhone.

**The Apple Watch app shows nothing.** Open Leaf on your iPhone once with the
watch nearby so the two can pair up.

**How do I delete my data?** Settings › Erase all data. See the
[privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) for what that covers.

**A book is missing or has the wrong page count.** Add it by hand from Search,
or open the book and edit its page count. Book data comes from
[Open Library](https://openlibrary.org).
