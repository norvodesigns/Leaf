# Leaf privacy policy

_Last updated: October 23, 2026_

Leaf is a reading planner for iPhone and Apple Watch. It has no accounts, no
analytics, no ads, and no servers of its own.

**What Leaf stores.** Your library (the books you add, your current page,
ratings), your reading sessions, your reading speed, and your settings. They
are kept on your iPhone, in the app's own storage, and, while iCloud is on for
Leaf, in your own private iCloud database under your Apple Account, so your other
devices stay in step. Leaf has no access to that database; only your devices do.
A summary of today's plan syncs directly to your paired Apple Watch over Apple's
WatchConnectivity framework. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.

**Book search.** When you search for a book that isn't in Leaf's built-in
catalog, the search text is sent to [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org), a
free, non-profit book catalog run by the Internet Archive, and cover images are
loaded from it. The request contains only the search text, not your identity or
your library. See Open Library's own privacy policy for how they handle requests.

**Scanning a book.** When you add a book by its barcode, the ISBN is looked up in
Leaf's built-in catalog and, if it isn't there, sent to Open Library in the same way.

**Camera and photos.** Leaf uses the camera only while you are looking at it: to
scan a book's barcode in Search, and to read a quote from the page you're holding
in a session note. The picture is read on your iPhone and never saved or sent.
Leaf asks to add to your photo library only when you save a quote card, and it
cannot see the photos already there.

**Apple Intelligence greetings.** If you turn them on, Leaf asks Apple's
on-device model to write the short greeting on Today. This runs entirely on your
iPhone. Nothing is sent to Leaf or anyone else.

**Reading circles.** Circles live in your library. If you choose to share a
circle, Leaf uses iCloud sharing: only the people you invite can see that circle,
and you can remove people or stop sharing at any time from the same sheet. The
people in a circle see the name you give it, your page in that circle's book, and
when your page moved over the last few weeks (for the standings). Nothing else
from your library is shared.

**Notifications.** Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They
are not push notifications from a server.

**Deleting your data.** Settings → Erase all data removes everything from that
device and its iCloud copy; your other signed-in devices keep their own copy and
may sync it back, so erase each one to remove your library everywhere. Deleting
the app removes the data from that device only; the iCloud copy stays and is
restored if you install Leaf again. You can also remove it in iOS Settings → your
name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

**Children.** Leaf collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
