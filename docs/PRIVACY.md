# Leaf privacy policy

_Last updated: October 15, 2026_

Leaf is a reading planner for iPhone and Apple Watch. It has no accounts, no
analytics, no ads, and no servers of its own.

**What Leaf stores.** Your library (the books you add, your current page,
ratings), your reading sessions, your reading speed, and your settings. All of
it stays on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. A summary of today's plan
syncs directly to your paired Apple Watch over Apple's WatchConnectivity
framework. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.

**Book search.** When you search for a book that isn't in Leaf's built-in
catalog, the search text is sent to [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org), a
free, non-profit book catalog run by the Internet Archive, and cover images are
loaded from it. The request contains only the search text, not your identity or
your library. See Open Library's own privacy policy for how they handle requests.

**Notifications.** Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They
are not push notifications from a server.

**Deleting your data.** Settings → Erase all data removes everything at once.
Deleting the app does the same.
